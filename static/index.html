How It Works

Understand the journey of an item from being reported to a happy reunion.

1. Report an Item

A user reports an item as 'Found' or 'Lost', providing a description, category, location, contact info, and an image.

2. AI Image Analysis

Our AI model generates a numerical signature (embedding) from the image, capturing its key visual features.

3. Smart Match Search

The AI instantly searches for visually similar items in the opposite category (e.g., searches Lost items if you Found one), filtering for a high confidence score.

4. Review and Claim

You are shown a list of potential matches. If you find yours, you can claim it with a single click.

5. Automated Notification

Once a claim is made, both parties automatically receive an email with each other's contact information to coordinate the return.

6. Mark as Returned

After the item is successfully returned, either party can mark it as 'Returned' to complete the item's journey.

7. Happy Reunion!

The owners connect and arrange for the safe return of the item. Mission accomplished!
